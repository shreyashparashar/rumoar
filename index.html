Ethos(03)

Designing a world, not just a product.

We don’t make bags. We make characters. The man who walks into the room with The Provocateur is not the man who leaves with The Drifter. RUMOAR is built for both of him, and for every version in between.

  1. 01

    Styled, not stocked.

    We design in outfits first and objects second. Every piece exists to finish a look, never to fill a shelf.

  2. 02

    Rare by design.

    Each design is made in small batches, so what you carry stays yours and doesn’t turn up on every street.

  3. 03

    One bag for every you.

    The Weekender, the Commuter, the Strategist. You are all of them by Friday. Drop 01 gives each one something to carry.
